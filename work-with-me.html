Process

How I work

No vague promises. Every engagement starts with agreement on the problem, timeline, and success condition.

1. Define the problem

Short discovery call or written brief. I’ll ask questions until the real problem is clear and measurable.

2. Agree scope and milestones

Outcome, timeline, communication cadence, and what I’ll deliver in writing.

3. Build or support

Execute the work, share progress, fix issues, and keep records you can reuse later.

4. Handover cleanly

Deliver outputs with concise documentation, next steps, and an honest review of what worked.

Response SLA

Availability & response

Clear expectations so you can plan confidently.

Response time

I aim to respond within 24 hours, usually sooner. If I’m unavailable, I’ll say so and suggest a backup option.

Communication channels

Engagement types

Freelance, contract, part-time, full-time, trainee placements, and one-off projects.
