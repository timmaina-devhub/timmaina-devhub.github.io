Module 01

Customer Churn Predictor

RFM features (recency, frequency, monetary + refund rate) fed into a logistic regression model tuned for recall. K-Means then groups customers into behaviorally distinct segments. Days since last purchase and refund rate came out as the two strongest churn signals.

Module 02

Fraud Detection

Behavioural features — transaction velocity, device count, IP repetition, and time-window patterns — fed into a Random Forest. SMOTE handled class imbalance. Transaction velocity in the past hour was the single strongest predictor, well ahead of transaction amount.

Module 03

Marketing ROI & Attribution

Markov Chain attribution distributes conversion credit across all channel touchpoints, not just the last click. A Random Forest then predicts campaign revenue. CPA was the dominant revenue predictor — more so than total spend or impressions.

Module 05

Credit Risk Assessment

The target variable was reconstructed from a composite risk score — the original default label had weak predictive signal. Debt-to-income and loan-to-income ratios were more informative than raw credit score. XGBoost reached F1 0.76 with ~77% recall and ~75% precision.

Module 06

Delivery Delay Predictor

Traffic congestion and weather risk were proxied from GPS speed patterns and historical delay data — no external APIs needed. Several early feature candidates had to be removed because they were effectively restatements of the target variable. Final R² of 0.70 after removing leaky features that had inflated it to ~0.99.
